GAME MODES
TEAM DEATHMATCHTwo teams of bots and you. First team to the kill limit wins
FREE-FOR-ALLEveryone for themselves. First to the kill limit wins
GUN GAMEEach kill gives you the next of 20 guns. Finish the ladder to win
HARDPOINTStand in the marked zone: a point a second while only your team holds it. It moves every minute
CAPTURE THE FLAGWalk over the enemy flag, bring it to your own flag. Walk over your dropped flag to return it
SEARCH & DESTROYOne life per round. Plant at A or B (or defuse), or wipe out the other team
SURVIVALThe original: endless waves, alone
